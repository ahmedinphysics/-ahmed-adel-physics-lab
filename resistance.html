💡 Key Concepts

  • Resistance depends on material, length, cross-sectional area and temperature.
  • Resistivity ρ is a material property; resistance R belongs to a particular object.
  • A longer wire has greater resistance, while a thicker wire has lower resistance.
  • For the metals shown, heating increases lattice vibration and usually increases resistance.
