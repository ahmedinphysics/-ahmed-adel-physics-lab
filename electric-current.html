💡 Key Concepts

  • Current is the rate of flow of electric charge, not the speed of individual electrons.
  • Without an electric field, electrons still move randomly but have zero net drift.
  • Applying a potential difference adds a tiny directed drift to the thermal motion.
  • The conventional-current direction is opposite to electron drift in a metal.
