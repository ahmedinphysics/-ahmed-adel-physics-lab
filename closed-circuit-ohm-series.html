💡 Key Concepts

  • In a series circuit, the same current passes through the battery and every lamp.
  • The battery's internal resistance causes an internal voltage drop Ir whenever current flows.
  • At fixed EMF, adding a lamp increases total resistance, so current and lamp brightness decrease.
  • At fixed current, adding a lamp requires a larger EMF; the simulation calculates it automatically.
  • Opening the main switch breaks the only path, so current becomes zero everywhere.
