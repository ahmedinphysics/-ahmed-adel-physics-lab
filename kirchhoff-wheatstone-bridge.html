💡 Key Concepts

  • A Wheatstone bridge has two divider branches connected by a fifth resistor R₅ between their midpoint nodes B and C.
  • Kirchhoff's Current Law must hold at both midpoint junctions: I₁ = I₃ + I₅ and I₂ + I₅ = I₄ using the stated current directions.
  • The bridge is balanced when VB = VC. Then ΔV across R₅ is zero, so I₅ = 0 even though R₅ is physically connected.
  • For ideal resistors, balance occurs when R₁/R₃ = R₂/R₄. This condition does not depend on the value of R₅.
  • Cross the balance point and I₅ changes sign. The animation reverses automatically to show the real current direction.
  • KVL still closes around the left, right and bridge-containing loops at every slider setting.

Σ Equations

(VB − Vₛ)/R₁ + VB/R₃ + (VB − VC)/R₅ = 0

(VC − Vₛ)/R₂ + VC/R₄ + (VC − VB)/R₅ = 0

I₁ = (Vₛ − VB)/R₁ · I₂ = (Vₛ − VC)/R₂

I₃ = VB/R₃ · I₄ = VC/R₄

I₅ = (VB − VC)/R₅

Balanced bridge: R₁/R₃ = R₂/R₄ ⇔ VB = VC ⇔ I₅ = 0

Model assumptions: ideal DC source, ideal wires, ohmic resistors, steady state, and no internal source resistance. The sign of I₅ is referenced to B → C.
