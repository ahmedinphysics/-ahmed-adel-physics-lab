💡 Key Concepts

  • Voltage is a comparison between two points; a single point needs a chosen reference.
  • One volt means one joule of potential-energy change per coulomb.
  • The electric field points from high potential toward low potential.
  • Changing the test charge changes ΔU, but it does not change ΔV.
