💡 Key Concepts

  • Kirchhoff's Voltage Law follows from conservation of energy in a lumped DC circuit.
  • Choose one loop direction and keep the sign convention consistent.
  • Crossing the source from − to + is a voltage rise: +V.
  • Moving through a resistor in the direction of current is a voltage drop: −IR.
  • In this series loop the same current passes through R₁ and R₂, but the larger resistance takes the larger share of the source voltage.
  • If the switch is opened, I = 0, so resistor drops become zero and the full source voltage appears across the open switch.

Σ Equations

ΣΔV = 0

+V − V₁ − V₂ = 0

I = V / (R₁ + R₂)

V₁ = IR₁

V₂ = IR₂

Ideal DC source, ideal wires, and ohmic resistors are used. With the switch open, the open gap carries the source voltage so KVL still closes around the defined loop.
