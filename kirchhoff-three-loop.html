💡 Key Concepts

  • Mesh-current analysis assigns one reference current to each independent loop. Here J₁, J₂ and J₃ are defined clockwise.
  • A negative mesh current does not mean the calculation failed. It means the actual current is opposite to the assumed clockwise reference.
  • A resistor shared by two meshes carries the algebraic difference of their mesh currents: I₄ = J₁ − J₂ and I₅ = J₂ − J₃ for the downward reference arrows shown.
  • If two adjacent mesh currents become equal, their shared resistor can remain physically connected while carrying zero current.
  • The three loop equations are coupled. Changing one source or one shared resistance can alter currents in all three meshes.
  • The source sliders are signed. A negative EMF means the battery polarity is reversed relative to the clockwise loop reference.
